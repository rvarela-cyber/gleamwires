⚠ What Is Wrong with Most Power Washing Vendors

They cut corners

Rent a machine from Home Depot, skip the gum, ignore corners, rinse the surface. Looks fine wet, looks the same when it dries.

They disappear after the invoice

No before/after photos, no sign-off docs, no QC. If something is wrong, they are already gone.

You never talk to anyone who matters

Aggregators post work orders and whoever picks it up first goes out. You get $15/hour dispatchers, not principals.

Cold water and cheap chemicals

No heated trucks. Harsh chemicals go down the storm drain. EPA fines hit the property owner, not the vendor.

They cannot scale

Fine for one location. Cannot handle 40 garages or 200 storefronts across multiple states.

No skin in the game

Every job is a one-and-done transaction. No relationship, no accountability, no reason to come back.
